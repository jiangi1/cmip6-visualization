Our Earth is warming

Since 1850, global temperatures have risen about 1.2°C. Scientists project continued warming through 2100, with the Arctic warming 2–3× faster than the rest of the world.

⚡

The good news? It depends on our emissions choices today.

Future warming varies dramatically — from 1.5°C to 4.5°C — depending entirely on which emission pathway humanity chooses to follow.
